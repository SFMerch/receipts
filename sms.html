SFM Receipts

Receipt Reminder Text Messages

Service First Merchandising, Inc. ("SFM") sends text messages to its own employees who hold an SFM company credit card. This page explains the program, how employees opt in and out, and how mobile numbers are handled.

What the messages are

When a purchase is made on an employee's SFM company card, SFM may send that employee a text reminding them to submit the receipt (and, for fuel purchases, the vehicle's mileage) in the SFM Receipts app. Messages contain the store name, amount and date of the purchase and a link to receipts.sfmerch.com. SFM may also send a short reminder about receipts that are still missing.

Sample message:
SFM Receipts: Card purchase at Cumberland Farms, $29.76 on 11/16. Please add your receipt: https://receipts.sfmerch.com Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

These are work-related account notifications only. SFM does not send marketing or promotional messages through this program.

Who receives them and how to opt in

Only SFM employees who hold an SFM company card receive these messages. Each employee opts in by giving SFM their mobile number and agreeing in writing to the following consent statement:

"I agree to receive text messages from Service First Merchandising, Inc. (SFM Receipts) at the mobile number I have provided, reminding me to submit receipts for purchases on my SFM company card. Message frequency varies with card use. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of employment."

Message frequency

Frequency varies with how often the card is used, typically one message per card purchase plus occasional reminders about missing receipts.

Opting out and help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will get one confirmation message and no further texts.
  • Reply START to resume.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact SFM at jason@sfmerch.com.

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

  • SFM collects employees' mobile numbers only to send the receipt reminder messages described on this page.
  • Mobile numbers and text message opt-in data are not sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Numbers are shared only with SFM's text message service provider, solely to deliver these messages.
  • Receipt photos, mileage and purchase details submitted in the SFM Receipts app are used only for SFM's bookkeeping and tax records and are available only to SFM management and SFM's accountant.
  • Questions or requests to remove your number: jason@sfmerch.com.
